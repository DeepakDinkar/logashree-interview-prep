## Questions to ask them
- What does the on-call rotation / incident process look like?

    Ans: Understanding the on-call rotation and incident response process is crucial for assessing how the team handles emergencies and maintains system reliability. It also provides insight into the expectations for engineers during high-pressure situations.

- How does the team handle post-mortems and learning from incidents?

    Ans: Understanding how the team conducts post-mortems and learns from incidents is important for assessing their commitment to continuous improvement and knowledge sharing. It also provides insight into the processes in place to prevent similar issues in the future.

- IaC maturity — fully Terraform? GitOps?

    Ans: Understanding the team's Infrastructure as Code (IaC) maturity is important for assessing their approach to managing infrastructure and deployments. It provides insight into the tools and practices they use, such as Terraform or GitOps, and how they ensure consistency, reliability, and scalability in their infrastructure.

- What's the biggest reliability/scaling challenge the team faces?

    Ans: Asking about the biggest reliability or scaling challenge the team faces provides insight into the current pain points and areas for improvement. It also allows you to understand the team's priorities and how they approach problem-solving in complex systems.
